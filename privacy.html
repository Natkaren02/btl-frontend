Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 May 2026

1. Who we are

BeyondTheLabel ("we", "us", "our") is a sustainable fashion discovery platform operated as a student project at the University of Copenhagen, Denmark.

For questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at: hello@beyondthelabel.dk

As we are based in Denmark, we are subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act.

2. What data we collect and why

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our service.

Account data
When you create an account: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash). Legal basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Profile and preferences
Size information, budget preferences, and sourcing preferences you choose to provide. Legal basis: contract. This data is necessary to personalise search results.

Wardrobe data
Items you manually add to your wardrobe tracker. This data is stored securely and is only accessible to you. Legal basis: contract.

Pinterest board URL
If you choose to connect a Pinterest board, we store the public board URL and process the images from that board for visual style matching. We do not store the Pinterest images themselves — they are processed in real time and immediately discarded. Legal basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting your board.

Search history
We store your search queries to improve recommendations. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can request deletion at any time.

Technical data
Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) retained for a maximum of 30 days for security purposes. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising purposes
  • We do not share your data with brands or retailers listed on the platform
  • We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects
  • We do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area (EEA)

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase on servers located in Stockholm, Sweden (EU North region). All data remains within the European Economic Area.

Our application backend is hosted on Railway.app on servers in the United States. However, your personal data is not stored on these servers — only processed transiently during requests. Railway is a data processor acting under our instructions.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • Wardrobe data: Until you delete the items or your account
  • Search history: 12 months, then automatically deleted
  • Server logs: 30 days
  • Pinterest board URL: Until you disconnect your board or delete your account

6. Your rights under GDPR

As a resident of the EU/EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: You can correct inaccurate data at any time via your account settings
  • Right to erasure: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • Right to restriction: You can ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@beyondthelabel.dk. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.dk.

7. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies required for the platform to function (session authentication). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

No cookie consent banner is required as we only use strictly necessary cookies, which are exempt from consent requirements under the Danish Cookie Order and ePrivacy Directive.

8. Third-party links

Our platform links to third-party websites including Vinted, DBA, Sellply, and brand websites. Once you leave BeyondTheLabel, this privacy policy no longer applies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

9. Children

BeyondTheLabel is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes by email. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
